# Buyer notes — oss-pocket

Signature: Mourad.Soltani

Prepared for one-to-one conversations only. Do not paste into a cold-email blast.

## Who it is for

EU-established solo sellers and very small shops (digital downloads, print-on-demand, small goods) who sell B2C into other EU countries and do not yet know if they have crossed the €10,000 Union threshold.

## What they buy

A local, auditable check they can run before talking to an accountant. Price anchor for a hosted version later: €9–€19/month for CSV upload, year lock, and a PDF pack the accountant can read. The repo itself is the free wedge.

## What it does not do

- No VIES lookup
- No VAT rate calculation
- No OSS XML filing
- No claim that the €10,000 figure is the only test (previous-year carryover still matters)

## One-sitting demo

1. Drop in their Shopify/Etsy export mapped to the six columns.
2. Run `report` for the current year.
3. Show excluded domestic and B2B rows so they trust the number.
4. If remaining is under €1,000, offer the accountant handoff note, not a filing upsell.

## Objection handling

"My platform already charges VAT." Platforms often collect marketplace VAT. Direct site sales still sit with the seller. Ask which channel is merchant-of-record before quoting a number.
